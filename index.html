Yasmiin Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting vulnerable communities through education, health, clean water, food security and humanitarian aid.

▷ Our Story

WHO WE ARE

Hope today. A better future tomorrow.

Yasmiin Foundation works with communities to deliver practical, dignified and sustainable support. We focus on people facing poverty, displacement, food insecurity and limited access to essential services.

Our approach puts communities at the center — listening, responding and building solutions that can last.
